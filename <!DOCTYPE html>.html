Common questions

I took a diploma. Can I still get a BE degree?

Yes. Diploma holders can write DCET and, with a good rank and the eligibility conditions met, join the 2nd year of BE or BTech directly. This is called lateral entry.

Can I switch streams after 10th if I choose wrong?

It is harder after PUC begins, so spend time on the quiz and talk to seniors first. Many careers are open through more than one stream, and degrees such as BCom and BSc can lead to a wide range of jobs.

Do I need a degree to build a good career?

Not always. ITI, diploma and skill certificates lead to well-paid technical work, and you can study further later. A degree opens more doors for some careers, such as medicine, law and most government services.

How do I pick between two courses I like?

Look at the first year syllabus of each, talk to a student or working person in both, and check which entrance exams and fees are involved. Choose the one you can imagine studying for the next three to five years.

Where can I find scholarships?

Start with the National Scholarship Portal and the Karnataka state scholarship portals, and ask your college office. Deadlines are strict, so check them early in the year.
